# ORBIT Heavy Launch Vehicle Specification

## 1. Vehicle Architecture
- **Vehicle Class**: Heavy-Lift Orbital Launch Vehicle (Tri-Core Liquid Propellant Architecture)
- **Total Height**: 70.0 meters
- **Gross Liftoff Mass**: 1,420,000 kg
- **First Stage Propulsion**: 27 Merlin-class vacuum-purged liquid oxygen / RP-1 kerosene engines
  - Center Core: 9-Engine Octaweb cluster (1 center gimbaled + 8 perimeter fixed)
  - Left Lateral Booster: 9-Engine Octaweb cluster
  - Right Lateral Booster: 9-Engine Octaweb cluster
- **Total Liftoff Thrust**: 22,819 kN (5,130,000 lbf) at sea level
- **Control Actuation**: 4 deployable cast titanium grid fins per core stage at interstage
- **Payload Fairing**: 5.2 meter diameter carbon-composite clamshell fairing with acoustic attenuation tiles

## 2. Structural Dimensions
- **Central Core Diameter**: 3.66 m
- **Booster Core Diameter**: 3.66 m each
- **Total Span**: 12.2 m across lateral boosters
- **Interstage Length**: 4.2 m
- **Fairing Height**: 13.1 m

## 3. Physical Materials (PBR)
- **Primary Hull**: Aluminum-Lithium alloy with ceramic white thermal barrier coating (Metallic: 0.15, Roughness: 0.25)
- **Interstage Band**: Unpainted autoclaved carbon-fiber composite (Metallic: 0.80, Roughness: 0.40)
- **Grid Fins**: Cast Titanium alloy with heat discolouration (Metallic: 0.90, Roughness: 0.30)
- **Engine Nozzles**: Inconel-718 regenerative cooling jacket (Metallic: 0.95, Roughness: 0.15)
